ℹ️ About Soft Delete & Recovery
How Soft Delete Works:
  • Soft Deletion: When accounts are deleted due to inactivity, they are marked as deleted but not permanently removed from the database.
  • 90-Day Grace Period: Deleted accounts remain recoverable for 90 days, allowing users or admins to restore them if needed.
  • Automatic Permanent Deletion: After 90 days, accounts are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
  • Data Preservation: While soft-deleted, all user data (children, assessments) is preserved but inaccessible.
Restoring an Account:
  • Click the "Restore" button next to any deleted account.
  • The account will be reactivated with is_active = 1
  • The last_login timestamp is set to now, giving them another 30-day period.
  • All warning flags are cleared.
  • The user can immediately log in again.
Warning Signs:
  • ⚠️ Accounts with 7 days or fewer until permanent deletion are highlighted in yellow.
  • Accounts overdue for permanent deletion (90+ days) will be removed in the next cleanup cycle.
